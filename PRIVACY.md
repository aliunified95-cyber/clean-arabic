# Privacy Policy — Clean Arabic by AM95

**Effective:** Upon publication of this page

This policy describes the data practices of the Clean Arabic by AM95 skill package, published by **AM95**. It applies to the skill and plugin files in this repository. It does not replace the privacy terms of the platform where you use the skill or the websites that host this repository.

## Data handled by the skill publisher

The package consists of written instructions and a reference list. It has no plugin server, external API, account system, analytics, telemetry, or file-upload feature. When used in ChatGPT, the skill publisher does not receive or have access to your prompts, conversations, or generated text through this package. The package does not independently collect or store that content.

## Platform processing

When you use the skill in ChatGPT, OpenAI processes your prompts and responses under the [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and the data controls for your account. Data handling, retention, training settings, and related rights are determined by OpenAI and your workspace/account configuration. See [ChatGPT Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt). AM95 cannot access, retrieve, or delete platform-held conversations.

The source files are hosted by GitHub. Visiting this repository or using its issue tracker is subject to [GitHub’s Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Support and privacy requests

For questions about this skill’s practices, use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/aliunified95-cyber/clean-arabic/issues); do not post personal or confidential information in a public issue. For requests concerning ChatGPT account data, contact OpenAI through its [Privacy Portal](https://privacy.openai.com/).

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be published in this repository with a corresponding commit history.
