# Arabic sources and evidence guide

Use references to support linguistic/lexical judgments. Project canon, brief, approved glossary, and audience decisions govern product choices.

## Contemporary Arabic and terminology
- [Riyadh Dictionary of Contemporary Arabic](https://dictionary.ksaa.gov.sa/) — contemporary standard Arabic lexicon from King Salman Global Academy for Arabic Language (KSAA).
- [KSAA digital platforms](https://ksaa.gov.sa/ar/platforms) — entry point for Suwar lexicographic resources, Falak corpora, language consultation, library, and other platforms.
- [Cairo Arabic Language Academy](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/) — Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, Al-Muʿjam al-Kabīr, terminology dictionaries, language rulings, and publications.
- [Sharjah Arabic Language Academy](https://www.alc.ae/) — consult its publications and initiatives; verify the specific reference and edition.
- For domain terms, consult relevant specialist standards/authorities as well as academy terminology; log the product decision in the glossary.

## Grammar, morphology, semantics, rhetoric
- Karin C. Ryding, *A Reference Grammar of Modern Standard Arabic* (Cambridge University Press, 2005) — broad reference for MSA syntax, morphology, and usage.
- Sībawayhi, *Al-Kitāb* — foundational classical grammar; use a reliable edition/commentary for advanced issues, not as the sole contemporary UI authority.
- Al-Khalīl ibn Aḥmad, *Kitāb al-ʿAyn* — foundational lexicography; distinguish historical from contemporary senses.
- Ibn Jinnī, *Al-Khaṣāʾiṣ* — classical linguistic theory and derivation.
- Ibn Mālik, *Alfiyyat Ibn Mālik* with a reputable commentary — traditional syntax and morphology; consult commentary for disputed points.
- ʿAbd al-Qāhir al-Jurjānī, *Dalāʾil al-Iʿjāz* and *Asrār al-Balāgha* — nazm, meaning relations, metaphor, rhetorical effect.
- For contemporary usage, test descriptive grammar claims against corpus evidence and peer-reviewed Arabic linguistics. Traditional grammarians may differ in analysis; do not report one analysis as universal without support.

## Corpora and NLP
- [KSAA platforms](https://ksaa.gov.sa/ar/platforms) includes Falak, a language-corpora platform; inspect genre, date, region, and annotation before generalizing.
- [Journal of Computational Linguistics and Arabic Language Processing](https://jclalp.ksaa.gov.sa/) — peer-reviewed work on Arabic NLP, morphology, syntax, semantics, dialects, diacritization, and evaluation.
- Treat automatic analyzers as aids only: segmentation, dialect coverage, names, ambiguity, and domain terms can cause errors.

## Localization evidence limits
No grammar book prescribes good game localization. Use project canon, terminology, platform constraints, and audience brief as primary evidence. “GCC” covers distinct markets and varieties; request a named market where relevant and validate sensitive choices with qualified local reviewers. For Lifuna, use client-supplied canon and approved terminology; this guide makes no independent claim about the game's world or official policy. When a choice may be contested, record the edition, page/entry, access date, and exact claim it supports.
